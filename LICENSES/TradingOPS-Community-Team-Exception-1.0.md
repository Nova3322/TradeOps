A Community Team may contain no more than three natural persons in total. Count

Each member must act solely in their individual capacity. No member may act for

For Member-Owned Trading, all capital must be beneficially owned solely by one

**Eligible Natural Person** means a natural person acting solely for themselves
and not for or on behalf of an Organization.

collaborating under the conditions in this exception.

**Member-Owned Trading** means using the Software to research, propose, review,
simulate, execute, monitor, or report trades made only with capital
beneficially owned by current Community Team members, for the benefit of those
members.

**Organization** means any legal entity, sole-proprietorship business,
partnership, company, corporation, fund, family office, studio, cooperative,
association, decentralized autonomous organization, nonprofit, charity,
educational institution, research organization, public body, government body,
