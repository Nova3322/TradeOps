# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in TradingOPS welcoming and harassment-free for
everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, precise, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and evidence, not people.
- Protect private trading, account, security, and identity information.
- Disclose conflicts of interest and do not pressure others to weaken safety
  controls or conceal incidents.
- Accept maintainer decisions made to protect users, licenses, and system safety.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, publishing credentials or private
financial information, sexualized conduct, impersonation, and sustained
disruption are prohibited.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns through the repository owner's private conduct or
security contact. Do not publish sensitive evidence in a public issue.
Maintainers may edit or remove content and temporarily or permanently restrict
participation. Enforcement decisions should be documented privately, applied
consistently, and protect reporters from retaliation.

This policy is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1. The canonical text is at
https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/.
